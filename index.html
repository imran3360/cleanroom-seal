What this proves — and what it doesn't. A provider_tee signature binds the reply to a model-serving TEE signer that matches the attestation report. A gateway signature binds the reply to the attested NEAR AI gateway only — it does not by itself prove model TEE execution, and this page labels it exactly that way.
